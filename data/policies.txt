SafeX policy summary

Orders are processed within 1 to 2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch and express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days. Standard shipping costs $5.99, is free for orders of $75 or more before tax, and express shipping costs $14.99.

SafeX ships within the United States only. Most products may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery when they are complete, undamaged, and in like-new condition. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after inspection.

Orders can be cancelled before fulfillment. SafeX accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and PayPal. SafeX-branded products have a one-year limited warranty for manufacturing defects under normal use.
